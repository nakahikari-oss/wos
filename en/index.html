Fishing Tournament Tips

Monthly 3-day event. Pro Angler (+10%) and Master Angler (+20%) packs are must-buys for score pushing; F2P can skip the Fishing Expert pack's gear-upgrade mats using accumulated supplies. Prioritize golden 5-star fish and drowning divers (+5–10% bonus), hitbox is on the head not the tail, Horn of Poseidon summons a golden fish at 300–350m, and Ice Fishing Voucher 5× mode can break 10K per run.

Farming Advanced Teleporters from the Nomadic Merchant

Top-left and top-center slots never drop Advanced Teleporters — only watch the other four. Three strategies by diamond budget: whale (skip low-discount books), dolphin (only buy ≤100-diamond items), F2P (free refreshes only). Compared to VIP shop (2,000 diamonds each, limited 2/week), the Nomadic Merchant is unlimited — worth farming every day.
