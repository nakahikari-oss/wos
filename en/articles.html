Wander Theater Complete Guide

LIVE — 11/11 run, 7 days　Expected value, spending tiers, and the exact order to buy things at the Bizarre Bazaar. Best play for every budget: only gamble at L2, don't push L8 (T=2 + L2 gamble yields 200 coins/talisman). Big changes this run: Fire Crystal down 24% (F2P can now max-buy), 1-hour Speedup flips from trap to midtier buy (5.7× cheaper), new Protected Exploration milestone bonus up to 168K coins. Includes full probability-driven floor pool analysis, CP ranking of all 18 Bazaar items (with item icons), and budget-by-budget shopping lists from F2P to $1,110 whale. Includes a shopping planner 🧮 that works out the cheapest spend for whatever you tick.

Silverfrost Shop Complete Guide

Ended — the 10/13–10/17 run　How many Silver Shells you get for free (125 over five days), how much each spending tier adds, and what every item in the shop is actually worth in real money. Key points: smaller packs are twice as efficient as large ones (20 Shells per dollar vs. 10), buying the $1 pack on all five days doubles your total for $5, and $455 is the cheapest route to the top 5,000 Star Point tier — $490 less than buying top-down. Fire Crystal has flipped from a trap to the cheapest channel in the game this run. Includes a shopping planner that works out the cheapest way to buy whatever you tick.

Journey of Light Complete Guide

Recurring event　A pure spending event with no skill element — only "where does the money go." The key is that packs are limited per event, not per day, which gives this event a hard ceiling: $390 buys everything and yields 948 expeditions. Includes cost-per-expedition comparisons (Team 2 at $0.13 is cheapest, Team 3 at $0.67 is actually the most expensive), a breakdown showing 87.5% of each pack is Charm materials, minimum cost per milestone, and the 2-expedition near-miss cliff before the $380 stopping point.

Fishing Tournament Tips

Monthly 3-day event. Pro Angler (+10%) and Master Angler (+20%) packs are must-buys for score pushing; F2P can skip the Fishing Expert pack's gear-upgrade mats by using accumulated supplies. Prioritize golden 5-star fish and drowning divers (+5–10% bonus), hitbox is on the head not the tail, Horn of Poseidon summons a golden fish at 300–350m, Ice Fishing Voucher 5× mode can break 10K per run, and if your gear isn't maxed, go to the Treasure Stage (550m depth, 20 fish per run).

Farming Advanced Teleporters from the Nomadic Merchant

Of the 6 Nomadic Merchant slots, the top-left and top-center never drop Advanced Teleporters — only watch the other four. Three strategies by diamond budget: whale (skip low-discount blue −7% / purple −6% books and random teleporters, buy everything else), dolphin (only buy ≤100-diamond items), F2P (free refreshes only). Compared to VIP shop (2,000 diamonds each, limited 2/week), the Nomadic Merchant is unlimited — worth farming every day.
