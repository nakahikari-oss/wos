The conclusion first: Journey of Light has almost no "skill" element — it is purely an event where you trade money for time. The only decision you control is how much you spend and, more importantly, what you spend it on. That answer is very clear, and it runs against most players' instincts.

$0.13 / expedition Cost per expedition from unlocking teams — 15–34× cheaper than buying packs ($2–$4.44 / expedition)

So the single most important sentence in this guide is: unlock all four teams before you even consider buying a pack. Full reasoning below.

Before we start: why this guide prices everything in USD

Because everyone's spending channel is different — direct card payments, top-up credits, regional platform discounts — so the local-currency amount people actually pay varies a lot. USD gives everyone a common yardstick. Every item value in this guide comes from this site's item baseline price table, which is reverse-engineered from regular cash-shop channels.

1. How the event works

The rules are simple — it's a production line:

Send a team on an Expedition → 8 hours later it returns with a Radiance Treasure → Expedition count goes up → Claim milestone rewards

Three moving parts:

  • Expedition: send a team out; it comes back 8 hours later carrying either a Common Radiance Treasure or a Premium Radiance Treasure.
  • Team slots: you start with 1 team and can unlock up to 4 (see the next section).
  • Pocket Watch: used on a team that is currently out. 1 Pocket Watch = 1 hour off the timer. You get 1 free per day; everything beyond that comes from packs.

The event runs for 5 days, giving each team a total of 120 hours. That 120-hour figure is the time budget for the whole event, and the starting point for every calculation below.

The fusing chain

Treasures you bring back can be fused upward:

  • 3 Common Radiance Treasures → 1 Premium Radiance Treasure
  • 3 Premium Radiance Treasures → 1 Exquisite or Dazzling Radiance Treasure (random)

2. The single most important thing: unlock all your teams

This is the only real decision point in the entire event, and the answer is unambiguous.

Here's why: every extra team adds another 120 hours to the event, which is 15 extra expeditions — and those 120 hours are free time that costs you zero Pocket Watches.

3. Converting Pocket Watches into expeditions

The math is simple: 1 Pocket Watch = 1 hour, so 8 Pocket Watches = a full 8-hour trip = 1 extra expedition.

Which gives the total-expeditions formula for the whole event:

Total expeditions = (Teams × 120 hours + Total Pocket Watches) ÷ 8

Using only the free daily Pocket Watch (5 over the event), here's what each team count gets you:

The free-to-play ceiling: as long as you have 12,000 Gems banked, you can reach 3 teams and 45 expeditions without spending any cash, claiming through the 4th milestone. Adding $5 for Team 4 pushes you to 60 expeditions and the 5th milestone. That $5 does far more work here than it would in any pack.

Is fusing worth it? There's a clear break-even point

The chain is 9 Common → 3 Premium → 1 Exquisite or Dazzling. Note that Exquisite/Dazzling is 75% / 25%, not an even split, so the expected value works out as:

Expected shards = 0.75 × 2 + 0.25 × 10 = 4 Mythic General Hero Shards

⚠️ Don't read that table at face value. It looks like "don't fuse" wins, but only because Mythic General Hero Shards have no regular cash-shop price and are counted as $0 — and they are the entire point of the fusing chain.

The real trade-off is: give up 1,800 Gems + 450 pts of Enhancement XP Components (about $1.36–$1.58) ⟷ get 4 Mythic General Hero Shards.

Break-even: each Mythic General Hero Shard needs to be worth at least $0.34–$0.40 (and that ignores the 450 pts of components, so the real bar is a bit higher).
In plain terms: do you need Mythic General Hero Shards? If yes, fuse. If no, just open the Commons and take the Gems.
Worth noting: the first fusing step (3 Common → 1 Premium) is a small net loss on its own. 3 Commons are 600 Gems + 150 pts components + 2.5 h speedups; 1 Premium is only 500 Gems + 200 pts components + 2 h speedups — you lose 100 Gems and 0.5 h of speedups to gain 50 pts of components (net loss roughly $0.13–$0.16). So if you don't intend to fuse all the way to Exquisite/Dazzling, stopping at Premium is the worst possible choice. Either fuse everything or fuse nothing.

Three numbers to remember:
  • $5 — unlock Team 4. The highest-return dollar in the event; every budget should spend it.
  • $30 — reach 150 expeditions for Mithril ×8. At 4.0–4.4×, this is the event's best sweet spot.
  • $340 — the cheapest way to complete the track (900). Spending past this is pointless; there's nothing left to claim.

8. Step-by-step routine

  1. The moment the event opens, spend Gems to unlock Team 3 (2,000 + 10,000 = 12,000 Gems). The earlier you unlock, the more of the free 120 hours you actually use.
  2. If you're spending anything at all, the first $5 goes to Team 4, not to a pack.
  3. Never leave teams idle. Each trip is 8 hours — send them all out before bed, and redeploy the moment you wake up. Every idle hour is a wasted Pocket Watch.
  4. Packs are daily-limited, so log in every day to buy ($5–$50 once each, $100 three times). Miss a day and that pack is gone for good.
  5. If you're buying packs, max the daily $5 first (best Watch efficiency), then add $10 and $20 if budget allows.
  6. Spend Pocket Watches on whichever team is closest to returning — completing an 8-hour block is a real extra expedition.
  7. Fuse or don't fuse based on whether you need Mythic General Hero Shards — if you do, fuse all the way to Exquisite/Dazzling; if not, open the Commons for Gems. Never stop at Premium, that's the worst outcome.
⚠️ The most common mistake: seeing a pack stuffed with Pocket Watches and buying it immediately. In reality every expedition bought through a pack costs 15–34× more than one from unlocking a team, and if you don't need Charm Design / Charm Guide, the packs' value collapses entirely. Do not buy a single pack until all four teams are unlocked.

9. TL;DR

This is a pure spending event. There's no skill — only the question of where the money goes.

  • Free-to-play: Gems to 3 teams → 45 expeditions → claim through the 30 milestone. If you're short on Gems, unlock what you can.
  • $5: unlock Team 4 → 60 expeditions → claim through 50. At 4.1–4.9×, this is the best $5 in the whole event.
  • $30: 4 teams + a $5 pack daily → 160 expeditions → Mithril ×8. At 4.0–4.4×, the best value tier.
  • $180: $5 + $10 + $20 daily → 560 expeditions, milestone 420. (Note that $130 also reaches 420 — see the minimum-cost table in section 7 if you want to save.)
  • $340: $5 ×5, $10 ×3, $20 ×4, $100 ×2 → exactly 900 expeditions, track complete. This is the stopping point; spending more accomplishes nothing.
  • Fusing: Exquisite/Dazzling is 75% / 25%; 9 Commons yield an expected 4 Mythic General Hero Shards. Break-even is $0.34–$0.40 per shard.

Before buying any pack, ask yourself one question: do I need Charm Design and Charm Guide? Yes → the packs are great value and the Pocket Watches are essentially free. No → unlock teams only and don't touch a single pack.

Next time Journey of Light runs, check whether the pack contents and milestone rewards have changed. The method in this guide still applies — just plug in the new numbers.
