Silverfrost Shop Complete Guide: Free Silver Shells, Spending Efficiency & Item Values

By Clarice Category: Events
LIMITED-TIME EVENT

Event period: 2026 / 10 / 13 – 10 / 17 (5 days)

The numbers on this page apply to this run of the event only. The Silver Shell price of every item in the Silverfrost Shop is re-tuned by the devs each time the event comes around, so do not carry these figures over to the next run.
(The previous run in September 2026 has been overwritten; what's preserved here is the analysis for 10/13 – 10/17.)

🧮 Want to know what it'll cost you? → Switch to the "Shopping Planner" tab below, tick the items you want, and it works out the cheapest way to buy them.

The Silverfrost Shop is a limited-time event store where you spend the event currency, Silver Shells, on items. This guide answers four questions: how many Silver Shells you can get without spending a cent, how many each spending tier gets you, how the second reward track — Star Points — should factor into your spending decisions, and roughly what each item in the shop is worth in real money.

1. How many Silver Shells can a free-to-play player get?

125 Silver Shells Total available to a zero-spend player across the whole event

Those 125 come from three free sources, none of which cost anything:

Free source Each time Times available Subtotal
Daily Missions 15 1×/day × 5 days 75
Free Silver Shell Pack 5 1×/day × 5 days 25
Event mission track "Jolly Journey" 25 One-off 25
Free-to-play total 125

In other words: log in each day, clear your Daily Missions, and grab the free pack while you're there, and you have a steady 20 per day. Add the one-off 25 from the "Jolly Journey" mission track and five days comes to 125.

Don't miss a claim: both the Daily Missions and the Free Silver Shell Pack reset daily — a day you don't claim is 20 Shells gone for good. Perfect attendance versus logging in only three days is a 40-Shell gap, and in this shop that is often the difference of one whole item.

⚠️ There's a 5-Shell source deliberately left out

The Daily Missions list also contains an entry reading "Make any payment 1 time(s)", worth another 5 Silver Shells. But completing it requires spending money, so it is no longer free-to-play — which is why the 125 above excludes it. Full rules in the next section.

2. How many more can spending get you?

Spending adds Silver Shells from two places: the "Make any payment" entry in the Daily Missions, and Frosty Wish, the pack that doubles your Daily Mission rewards. The two stack.

a. "Make any payment" — spend once a day for 5 extra Shells

Read this rule carefully: make one payment per day, claim 5 Shells, and it resets the next day. It is not a one-time unlock that then feeds you automatically — you have to make a payment on each of the five days to collect the full 5 × 5 = 25.

The good news is that the bar is very low: any paid pack counts, with no minimum spend. So the cheapest way to tick it off is to buy the $1 Common Silver Shell Pack every day — $1 gets you the pack's own 20 Shells plus the 5 from the mission.

The most commonly missed point: if you were going to spend on this event anyway, split your budget across all five days instead of buying everything on day one. The same money spread over five days earns you an extra 25 Silver Shells; spent all at once on day one, those 25 become just 5.

b. "Frosty Wish" — doubles your Daily Mission rewards

Frosty Wish is an event-only pack, priced at $5, that doubles every Daily Mission reward once purchased:

  • Free portion of the Daily Missions: 15 → 30
  • The "Make any payment" portion: 5 → 10
  • Daily Missions in total go from 20 to 40 per day

It also increases the rewards from the "Jolly Journey" mission track — but note that the Silver Shells from "Jolly Journey" stay at 25 and do not increase; what goes up is everything other than the Shells.

Frosty Wish is itself a paid pack, so buying it already satisfies that day's payment requirement — you don't need to buy anything else on day one.

If you're buying it, buy it early: Frosty Wish earns its value by doubling Daily Missions, and Daily Missions reset every day. Buy on day one and all five days are doubled; wait until day four and you only get two days of doubling. The later you buy, the fewer Shells the same $5 returns.

⚠️ Key point: the more expensive the pack, the worse the value

This runs completely counter to the usual mobile-game instinct, so pay close attention to the "Shells per $1" column:

  • The $1 and $2 tiers: 20 Shells per dollar — the best rate in the event
  • The $5 tier drops to 16 per dollar
  • The $10 tier drops to 12 per dollar
  • Everything $20 and above is just 10 per dollar — half the efficiency of the smallest pack

Put another way, buying Shells through the $100 Mythic pack means paying double for the same thing. Whatever your budget, always start from the smallest pack and work upward.

💡 The best value play: $1 a day

Put all the rules above together and one absurdly efficient play falls out: buy nothing but the $1 Common Silver Shell Pack, once a day, for five days.

For $5 total you get:

  • The packs themselves: 20 × 5 days = 100
  • Unlocking the "Make any payment" Daily Mission: 5 × 5 days = 25
  • The free sources you'd have had anyway: 125
$5 takes you from 125 to 250 Silver Shells — a straight doubling

That $5 bought 125 Silver Shells, i.e. 25 per dollar — better than any pack in the store (the best pack only manages 20 per dollar). The reason is that it exploits two mechanics at once: small packs having the best rate, and the daily payment mission handing over 5 Shells.

If you only ever want to spend money on this event once, this is the play.

d. Is Frosty Wish ($5) actually worth buying?

Frosty Wish doubles your Daily Missions, but how much it doubles depends on whether you make a payment each day — the 5 Shells from "Make any payment" only exist if you've paid, and doubling needs something to double. So the answer splits in two:

  • If you will pay every day: Daily Missions go from 20 to 40 per day, i.e. 100 extra Shells over five days. $5 for 100 Shells = 20 per dollar, matching the best pack rate in the event, and you also get everything non-Shell doubled on top. Worth buying.
  • If this $5 is all you'll spend and you won't pay on the other days: only the free 15 gets doubled, giving 85 extra Shells over five days — a rate of 17 per dollar. Purely in Shell terms, you'd be better off splitting that $5 into $1-a-day pack purchases (which is 25 per dollar).

3. Don't forget the Star Points reward track

The event has a second progress track called Star Points, which pays out a reward each time you hit a listed score. It's almost welded to your Silver Shell spending, so factor it in before you spend — otherwise it's very easy to end up just short of a threshold that a tiny bit more spending would have cleared.

How Star Points are earned

Source Points Notes
Event mission track "Jolly Journey" 50 For completing the whole track; one-off
Daily Missions 10 /day Just for logging in, no spending needed. With Frosty Wish this becomes 20 /day
Silver Shell packs 1 pt per Shell However many Shells a pack gives, that's how many points you get

Note that last row: only Shells from paid packs award Star Points — free Shells from missions don't count. So Star Points are essentially just another way of writing "how much money you spent".

Spending nothing, five days gives you 50 (Jolly Journey) + 10 × 5 (Daily Missions) = 100 points; with Frosty Wish it's 50 + 20 × 5 = 150.

🎯 The cheapest route to 5,000 Star Points

The principle first: buying Frosty Wish gives you a 150-point base (50 from Jolly Journey + 20 × 5 days of Daily Missions), leaving 4,850 points to cover with packs. Since packs award points equal to the Shells they give, the question becomes "what's the cheapest way to buy 4,850 Silver Shells?"

4. What are the shop's items worth?

Once you know how Silver Shells are acquired, you can flip the question around: that item priced at 3,000 Shells — what does it actually cost in money?

What one Silver Shell costs

Because pack rates differ, a Silver Shell has no single price — it has a range:

Scenario Cost per Shell Notes
Cheapest possible $0.05 Buying only the $1 and $2 tiers (20 per dollar)
Buying in bulk $0.10 Every pack $20 and above is only 10 per dollar
If you only want to remember one number, remember $0.10. The $0.05 rate is capped at $3 a day (the $1 and $2 Common packs), which is a very small volume; anyone genuinely stockpiling Shells spends the overwhelming majority of their money at 10 per dollar. Estimating with the upper bound is closer to what you'll really pay, and keeps you from overestimating what you can afford.

Two bargains and one trap

🔥 Bargain ① of this run: Fire Crystal has flipped completely and is now the cheapest channel in the game. Last run, Silverfrost Fire Crystal cost 1.5–3× the regular channel and I flagged it "do not buy here". This run is the exact opposite — the main-shop ×20 / 10 Shells works out to $0.025–$0.05 each, which is 1.5–3× cheaper than the cheapest regular channel (Mix & Match, $0.0744 each); the Daily Limit ×70 / 30 Shells is marginally better still. Combined limits: you can buy 1,350 Fire Crystals this event (1,000 from the main shop + 350 from the Daily Limit section). If you're stockpiling Fire Crystal, max this out.

✅ Bargain ②: Mystery Badge is still worth buying here. This is one of the few items on the list that maps directly onto a regular in-game price. The shop gives 500 for 10 Shells, which is about $0.001–$0.002 each; the regular pack is 2,500 for $5, i.e. $0.002 each. Buying in the Silverfrost Shop is at worst the same as normal, and at best half price. Maxing the 30-purchase limit takes 300 Shells for 15,000 badges — a good opportunity if you're stockpiling them.

⚠️ The trap: the 1-Shell 5-minute speedup is actually worse value than the 10-Shell 1-hour speedups. Work out how much speedup time each Shell buys:

  • 5m General Speedup ×5 = 25 min ÷ 1 Shell = 25 min per Shell
  • 1h General Speedup ×8 = 480 min ÷ 10 Shells = 48 min per Shell
  • 1h Research / Training Speedup ×9 = 540 min ÷ 10 Shells = 54 min per Shell

That 1-Shell item has the lowest price tag and a 999 limit, so it looks like a handy way to spend loose change — but it's actually half as efficient as the 1-hour packs. If you want speedups, buy the three 10-Shell ones.

Four things to take away from that table

  • The big surprise this run is still Fire Crystal — 1.55× per unit, and it was a trap (0.5×) last run. Biggest swing on the list; buy it first. Steel (1.33×), Mithril (1.25×) and Design Plans / Polishing Solution / Hardened Alloy (1.12×) are also genuinely cheaper here.
  • Essence Stones and Lucky Hero Gear Chest dropped from "break-even / slightly pricey" to "pricey" (0.83× and 0.78×), because their regular channel got 17% cheaper on S7. Charm Guide / Charm Design went the same way (0.83×) — not because they got more expensive, but because a cheaper channel turned up: Artisan's Trove, whose $5 tier gives 48 Charm Guides + 48 Charm Designs, i.e. $0.0521 each, another 19–25% below the Custom Artistry Chest that used to be the baseline. If you need charm materials, buy them from Artisan's Trove instead.
  • Charm Secrets is still slightly good value, but only just (1.02–1.07×). There is no regular channel that prices Charm Secrets directly — the figure has to be reverse-derived from a pack's contents, so this row carries a wider error margin than any other. Its break-even point is $2.00 each and the estimate lands at $2.03–2.14, i.e. it scrapes through. Buy it if you want, but don't treat it as one of this run's headline bargains.
  • Pet advancement materials and Advanced Wild Mark are still traps (0.56× and 0.42×). The same money gets you roughly twice to two-and-a-half times as many from the Custom Pet Chest, so keep not buying these two in the Silverfrost Shop.

How should a free-to-play player spend their 125?

125 Shells doesn't stretch far — against the table above, the 250- and 400-Shell items are completely out of reach, and even the 50-Shell row only affords two purchases. So the approach is to rule out the bad value first, then pick from whatever you're currently shortest on:

  • Definitely rule out: pet advancement materials and Advanced Wild Mark (both noticeably pricier than regular channels), plus the 1-Shell 5-minute speedup (half the efficiency of the 1-hour packs)
  • The top pick this run is Fire Crystal: the main-shop ×20 is only 10 Shells — the value king of the whole shop. The Daily Limit ×70 is only 30 Shells, once per day. If you're stockpiling Fire Crystal, taking all five Daily Limit purchases costs 150 Shells for 350 Fire Crystals — that's basically your entire budget, but it's a serious haul
  • Next best are Steel and Mithril: Steel ×20,000 is only 10 Shells (1.33×) and Mithril is 40 Shells each (1.25×). If you're not going all-in on Fire Crystal, these two are a good way to spread the budget. Charm Secrets (1.02–1.07×) is still good value too, just by a thin margin, so it ranks behind these two.
  • The 10-Shell row is the most flexible: at 10 a go, 125 Shells covers 12 purchases, letting you top up exactly what you're missing. Mystery Badge, all three 1-hour speedups, and Design Plans / Polishing Solution / Hardened Alloy / Steel all live on this row
  • Be careful with the 50-Shell items: two purchases eat 80% of your budget, so only go there if it's precisely the material you're stuck on

What you end up buying still depends on where you're personally bottlenecked — the points above just clear the obviously bad value out of the way; pick from the rest according to your own needs.

TL;DR

Free-to-play: log in daily, clear your Daily Missions, claim the Free Silver Shell Pack — 125 Shells over five days. Each day missed costs you 20.

Spending a little: buy the $1 Common Silver Shell Pack on all five days. That $5 takes you from 125 to 250 Shells, the most efficient spend in the event.

Spending more: smaller packs are better value ($1 and $2 are 20 per dollar; $20 and up is only 10 per dollar), so always buy cheapest-first. If you want Frosty Wish ($5), buy it on day one, and split your budget across all five days so you collect the 5 Shells from the daily payment mission.

Check Star Points before you spend: thresholds are 50 / 200 / 400 / 1,000 / 2,000 / 5,000. Frosty Wish alone puts you at 150, and just $3 more clears the 200 tier. This run's rewards are in the "Reward thresholds" section above; in short — 50 is effectively free, 400 isn't good value, and if you're pushing, push to 5,000 (Custom Mythic Hero Gear Chest ×3).

To reach 5,000: buy this list — 5× each of the $1, $2, $5 and $10 packs, 3× the $20, 2× the $50, 2× the $100, plus Frosty Wish. $455 total gets you 5,050 points. Doing it backwards from the big packs costs $945, nearly $500 more.

Biggest surprise this run: Fire Crystal has flipped. Main shop ×20 for 10 Shells, Daily Limit ×70 for 30 Shells once a day — 1.5–3× cheaper than regular channels. If you stockpile Fire Crystal, max out both limits.

Other good value: Steel (1.33×), Mithril (1.25×), Design Plans / Polishing Solution / Hardened Alloy (1.12×), Charm Secrets (1.02–1.07×, thin margin). Don't buy: Pet Advancement Materials Custom Chest, Advanced Wild Mark, and the 1-Shell 5-minute speedup.
